The Options Map

The real ways to stop a foreclosure, and the catch with each

Every path below has trade-offs. Knowing them early is how you avoid losing everything at the auction. The most expensive mistake is waiting until there’s no time left to choose.

1
Reinstatement
Keep the home
What it isPay all the past-due amounts in one lump sum to bring the loan current.
What it takesThe full amount you’re behind, plus fees, all at once, before the sale.
The catchIf the reason you fell behind hasn’t changed, you can end up right back here.
2
Loan Modification
Keep the home
What it isThe lender agrees to change your loan terms so the payment is more manageable.
What it takesVerifiable income and weeks to months of paperwork with the lender.
The catchThe new payment can still jump, and a lot of modifications re-default within a year or two if the underlying budget doesn’t actually work.
3
Cash Sale (as-is)
Protect your equity
What it isSell the home quickly and as-is before the sale date — no repairs, no agent fees. This is the path where we can buy it from you directly.
What it takesA buyer ready to close fast (that can be us).
The catchYou trade top-dollar for speed and certainty — usually a bit under full open-market price, but it’s done and the equity is protected.
4
Sell on the Open Market
Protect your equity
What it isStop the foreclosure, list the home, sell for the most you can, and walk away with your equity.
What it takesEnough time before the auction, and someone to coordinate it.
The catchOnly works if you act before the window closes — the closer to the sale, the harder it gets.

If you want to keep the home

Reinstatement and modification can work, but only if the income problem that put you behind is genuinely solved. Otherwise they often just delay things.

If keeping it isn’t realistic

Selling before the auction is how you protect the equity you’ve built, instead of handing it to the bank at the courthouse steps.

⏳ Every option above has a clock on it

On the day of the auction, any equity left in the home can be lost for good. Each path here only exists while there is still time on the clock — which is exactly why it’s worth figuring out your real timeline now, not later.

Then reinstatement probably isn’t the path, and that’s okay — it’s only one of four. Selling before the auction can still protect your equity even when catching up isn’t possible. The point is to choose the option that fits your reality.
